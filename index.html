Tabby Jameiyah جمعية تابي

Save together. Take turns. Skip the chasing.

A savings circle (jameiyah) that Tabby runs for you. The organizer sets the monthly amount, the members and a payday collection date. Tabby then handles the collections and payouts, with an optional protection plan for when someone misses a payment. No spreadsheet, no chasing, no interest.

Split a purchase with someone right at checkout. Each share becomes its own Tabby plan, paid now or in 4, with flows for declined, expired and not-approved cases.

Research · SDAIA–KFUPM JRCAI · 2026

Pixels or Prior?

Do small vision-language models actually see the medical devices in a chest X-ray, or do they guess from what usually appears?

I built a benchmark of same-patient chest X-ray pairs to measure medical-device hallucinations, evaluated seven open VLMs across four device categories, and tested abstention and expert-logit fusion as mitigations. The research report is being prepared for submission to JURI.

Research · University of Glasgow

CaLT: Calibrated Location Tensors

Predicting which camera a pedestrian appears in next, and where, by correcting defects in a published model (SMO-MCTF) and in its evaluation. On WNMF under matched evaluation, average position error fell from 124.1 to 61.5 px and camera-gated box IoU rose from 0.13 to 0.63 (day-disjoint 5-fold CV, 190,663 windows).

  • Forecasting
