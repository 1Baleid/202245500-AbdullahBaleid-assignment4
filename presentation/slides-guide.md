## RECORDING TIPS

1. **Screen Recording**: Use OBS Studio (free) or Windows Game Bar (Win+G)
2. **Show your face**: Small webcam overlay in corner adds professionalism
3. **Practice**: Run through 2-3 times before recording
4. **Live Demo**: Have the website open and ready before recording
5. **Pace**: Speak clearly, not too fast
6. **Konami Code**: Demo the easter egg - it's impressive!
